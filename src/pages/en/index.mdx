Starting in print, I made the transition to the web at the turn of the millennium. I learned HTML and CSS autodidactically—long before YouTube or suitable degree programs. Instead of dry theory, my focus was on hands-on practice: as a full-stack developer, I covered concept, design, frontend, and backend for years. This curious mindset and looking beyond the tellerrand still drives me today—all the way to agentic coding.

This drive showed early on: as a digital pioneer, I was among the first to shape podcasting in Germany with the launch of "Technikwürze" in 2005. What started as a one-man show quickly evolved into a collaborative format: by purposefully involving external experts, I drove the democratization of technical knowledge—and learned that the best results always arise through collaboration.

This principle still applies today. I work at the intersection of design and deployment—bringing people, ideas, and code together with great UX. Since 2015, I have also been lecturing at the university, showing students how modern websites are built—mobile-first, responsive, and frustration-free.
