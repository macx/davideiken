Starting in print, I made the transition to the web at the turn of the millennium. I taught myself HTML and CSS -- long before YouTube or suitable degree programs. As a full-stack developer, I then covered concept, design, frontend, and backend for years. This view of the whole picture is the foundation I build on today.

My drive has always been intrinsic: as a **digital pioneer**, I launched 'Technikwürze' in 2005, one of the first German podcasts, and opened it up to external experts early on. For 11 years, I gave students at HAWK the tools to build modern websites. Both taught me: the best results arise through collaboration.

This is exactly the experience that pays off today. My frontend engineering is built on [agentic coding](/en/engineering/#ai-native-frontend-engineering): AI agents write the code, I take responsibility for architecture, UX, and quality. At the intersection of design and deployment, I bring people, ideas, and code together into a thoughtful user experience: mobile-first, responsive, and frustration-free.
