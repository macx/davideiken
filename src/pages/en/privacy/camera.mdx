<section class="section-surface">

    <h2>1. Purpose of the Construction Camera</h2>
  </div>
  <p>
    The images from the construction camera are <strong>not publicly accessible</strong>. They are strictly private and are recorded exclusively on our own property. The use of the camera is to exercise our domiciliary rights and serves the following purposes:
  </p>
  <ul>
    <li>Prevention of unauthorized access</li>
    <li>Protection against theft and prevention of vandalism</li>
    <li>Surveillance for accident prevention on the construction site</li>
  </ul>
</section>

<section class="section-projekte">
  <div class="section-header">
    <h2>2. Privacy Information</h2>
  </div>
  
  <h3>Data Controller</h3>
  <p>
    The operator of the construction camera is responsible for compliance with all data protection regulations in accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).
  </p>

  <h3>Type of Data Collected</h3>
  <ul>
    <li>Images of the construction site</li>
    <li>Timestamps of the images</li>
    <li>Technical metadata (e.g., camera settings)</li>
  </ul>

  <h3>Legal Basis</h3>
  <p>
    The processing is based on Art. 6 Para. 1 lit. f GDPR (legitimate interest). Our legitimate interest consists in the protection of our property (protection against theft and vandalism) as well as accident prevention and the exercising of our domiciliary rights.
  </p>

  <h3>Retention Period</h3>
  <p>
    The recordings are stored for the duration of the construction project (subject to any statutory retention periods) and subsequently deleted.
  </p>

  <h3>Your Rights</h3>
  <p>As a data subject, you have the following rights:</p>
  <ul>
    <li>Right of access (Art. 15 GDPR)</li>
    <li>Right to rectification (Art. 16 GDPR)</li>
    <li>Right to erasure (Art. 17 GDPR)</li>
    <li>Right to restriction of processing (Art. 18 GDPR)</li>
    <li>Right to data portability (Art. 20 GDPR)</li>
    <li>Right to object (Art. 21 GDPR)</li>
  </ul>
</section>

<section class="section-surface">
  <div class="section-header">
    <h2>3. Technical Security Measures</h2>
  </div>
  <p>To protect the data as best as possible, we employ the following measures:</p>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>Encryption:</strong> All data transmissions are encrypted.</li>
    <li><strong>Access Control:</strong> Only authorized persons have access to the camera and the recordings.</li>
    <li><strong>Secure Storage:</strong> The data is stored on secure servers.</li>
    <li><strong>Logging:</strong> Any access is logged and monitored.</li>
  </ul>
</section>
