<section class="section-surface">
  <div class="section-header">
    <h2>1. Purpose of the Camera(s)</h2>
  </div>
  <p>
    The images from our camera(s) are <strong>not publicly accessible</strong>. They are strictly private and are recorded exclusively within the boundaries of our own property. <strong>Public paths, sidewalks, the street, and neighboring properties are not captured.</strong> The use of the camera(s) is to exercise our domiciliary rights and serves the following purposes:
  </p>
  <ul>
    <li>Prevention of unauthorized access</li>
    <li>Protection against theft and prevention of vandalism</li>
    <li>Surveillance for accident prevention during the construction phase</li>
    <li>Ongoing security of the property after the house has been completed</li>
  </ul>
  <p>
    During the construction phase, the camera also serves to document construction progress (time-lapse recording). Once the house is completed and occupied, the camera continues to operate solely for security purposes.
  </p>
</section>

<section class="section-projekte">
  <div class="section-header">
    <h2>2. Privacy Information</h2>
  </div>
  
  <h3>Data Controller</h3>
  <p>
    The operator of the camera(s) is responsible for compliance with all data protection regulations in accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).
  </p>

  <h3>Type of Data Collected</h3>
  <ul>
    <li>Images of the property</li>
    <li>Timestamps of the images</li>
    <li>Technical metadata (e.g., camera settings)</li>
  </ul>

  <h3>Legal Basis</h3>
  <p>
    The processing is based on Art. 6 Para. 1 lit. f GDPR (legitimate interest). Our legitimate interest consists in the protection of our property (protection against theft and vandalism), accident prevention during the construction phase, and the ongoing exercising of our domiciliary rights after the house is completed.
  </p>

  <h3>Retention Period</h3>
  <p>
    During the construction phase, recordings are stored for the duration of the construction project (subject to any statutory retention periods) due to the additional documentary time-lapse recording of construction progress, and are subsequently deleted.
  </p>
  <p>
    In regular operation after the house is completed and occupied, recordings from the security camera(s) are automatically deleted after <strong>72 hours</strong>. Longer storage only occurs if the recordings are required to investigate a specific incident (e.g., burglary, property damage), such as for submission to law enforcement authorities.
  </p>

  <h3>Your Rights</h3>
  <p>As a data subject, you have the following rights:</p>
  <ul>
    <li>Right of access (Art. 15 GDPR)</li>
    <li>Right to rectification (Art. 16 GDPR)</li>
    <li>Right to erasure (Art. 17 GDPR)</li>
    <li>Right to restriction of processing (Art. 18 GDPR)</li>
    <li>Right to data portability (Art. 20 GDPR)</li>
    <li>Right to object (Art. 21 GDPR)</li>
  </ul>
</section>

<section class="section-surface">
  <div class="section-header">
    <h2>3. Technical Security Measures</h2>
  </div>
  <p>To protect the data as best as possible, we employ the following measures:</p>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>Encryption:</strong> All data transmissions are encrypted.</li>
    <li><strong>Access Control:</strong> Only authorized persons have access to the camera(s) and the recordings.</li>
    <li><strong>Secure Storage:</strong> The data is stored on secure servers.</li>
    <li><strong>Logging:</strong> Any access is logged and monitored.</li>
  </ul>
</section>
